FirstPet Prototypes

FirstPet is a readiness check for first-time pet adopters. We built three prototypes, each testing a different hypothesis with a different persona. Pick one and click through, no tour needed.

A

Shared Check

Persona: Priya, a first-time co-owner watching her budget.

Hypothesis: A free readiness check shared with a partner is enough that at least 1 in 10 people who finish it pick a premium plan.

Try: Start a check as Priya, share it with Marcus, and get your verdict.

B

Pro Network

Persona: Dana, a partnership lead at a pet care business.

Hypothesis: Professionals want introductions to prepared, consenting households and will pay an annual membership instead of paying per lead.

Try: Review a pending introduction, then look at membership.

C

Shelter Hand-off

Persona: Carla, an adoption coordinator who wants fewer returns.

Hypothesis: Shelters make the free check step one of adoption if it reaches them before the visit, and may pay for analytics across adoptions.

Try: Open an upcoming visit as the counselor, then request a check for an applicant.
