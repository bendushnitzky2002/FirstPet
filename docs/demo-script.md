# Demo script

Two minutes per prototype. List the clicks in order, and what we say at each one.

## A: Shared Check

Link: https://dushben.github.io/FirstPet/a/

Backup: on the landing page, press **d** three times (not in a text box). That loads a demo check for Priya and Marcus and jumps straight to the side by side screen. Use it for rehearsal, or if the live flow breaks. "Start over" on the verdict screen clears it.

Live path (about two minutes):

- [ ] Landing: "We never ask for an account. The answer can be not yet." Click **Start the check**.
- [ ] Question 1: type "Priya", pick **Family with kids**. "One person can start alone." Click through the next six questions quickly (Dog, ZIP 27514, about 4 hours, 1 to 3 nights, Fine, Time and The kids).
- [ ] Invite screen: point at the big code and **Copy link**. "Marcus only sees the money and schedule questions." Click **Open the link in a new tab**.
- [ ] Partner tab: type "Marcus", answer 9 hours, 1 to 3 nights, Tight but doable, Cost and Time. Click **See what we both said**.
- [ ] Side by side: "This is the screen the hypothesis lives on." Point at a teal match, then an orange **Talk about this** row and its nudge. Scroll to the cost block and the required cushion.
- [ ] Click **See our verdict**: "Ready once a few things change," with a checklist in their words. Tick one item.
- [ ] Under the verdict, point at **Want help after adoption day?**: "This is our real test. We need at least 10% of households that finish to say yes." Click **Yes, I would**.
- [ ] Click **Save this as a one-page summary** to show the printable page.
- [ ] To show the numbers, open `#/results` (for example https://dushben.github.io/FirstPet/a/#/results): completed checks, yes, no, and the share against 10%. Results live in this browser only; **Clear results** resets them before a test session.

If asked about the solo case: on the invite screen, **Preview the result without them** gives a verdict with a banner saying it is based on one person's answers.

## B: The Number

Link: https://dushben.github.io/FirstPet/b/

Backup: on the profile page, press **d** three times (not in a text box). That loads a demo state (one introduction accepted, one passed) and jumps to Introductions with the membership nudge showing. Open `#/log` to see what a session recorded, and **Reset the demo** there between testers.

Live path (about two minutes):

- [ ] Profile: "This is what a pro sees first: their profile exactly as owners see it." Point at the vetted badge and what it means, then the list sorted by distance only and the "No ranking. No paid placement." box.
- [ ] Click **See introductions**: four households, each with a verdict and the need in their words. Point at what Priya and Marcus shared (everything) and what Taylor shared (only the verdict, the rest locked).
- [ ] Accept Priya and Marcus. Pass on Taylor and pick **We would need to see more first**, then type what you would want. "That answer is one of the things we want to learn."
- [ ] Click **See membership** in the nudge: two yearly tiers, no fee per lead, prices labeled as placeholders.
- [ ] Click **Join the network** on Clinic. It records the click and asks one question: "What would make our service worth it?" Type an answer and send.
- [ ] If asked how we test the opposite: the "Would you rather pay per introduction?" link records that preference the same way. Open `#/log` to show both.

## C: Shelter Hand-off

Link: https://dushben.github.io/FirstPet/c/

Backup: on the landing page, press **d** three times (not in a text box). That loads the sample shelter plus Dana Whitfield, who just finished a check for Biscuit and visits today, and jumps to Upcoming visits. "Reset sample data" on the landing page goes back to the plain sample list (Subscribe Now clicks and answers are kept).

Live path (about two minutes):

- [ ] Landing: "The check is free, and it's the shelter's step one." Click **I'm an adoption counselor**.
- [ ] Upcoming visits: "This is Carla's day. Every visit already has a result and what's missing." Point at Priya and Marcus: **Ready once** and three orange **Missing** flags.
- [ ] Request a check: type a name, keep Biscuit and tomorrow at 10:00, click **Request a check**. "One click, and the link is copied to send." Open the copied link in the address bar (or click **Open as the adopter** on Chris Alvarez).
- [ ] Adopter side: the start screen greets them by name with the visit time and the four steps. Click **Start the check** and answer quickly (Me and a partner, We rent and haven't asked yet, 8 hours, "me mornings, Sam evenings", No plan yet, Tight but doable, The cost). Click **See my result**, then **See what the counselor sees**.
- [ ] Readiness summary: **Missing before the visit** at the top. Click **Ask before the visit**: "We draft the message; Carla copies it into her email." Click **Copy message and log it**. Then the verdict and **Talk about these at the counter**. Mention **One-page summary** for the file.
- [ ] Click **Week-two check-ins** (or **Check-ins** on a phone): "Two weeks after adoption we check in. Taylor Brooks is flagged **Call this week**." Open Taylor: the check-in is at the top with the plan versus week two table. Click **I called**: Taylor drops off the call list.
- [ ] Click **Plans**: "All of this is free. What a shelter might pay for is analytics across adoptions and locations." Click **Subscribe Now**, answer "What would make our service worth it?", and point at the count at the bottom: that's our test.

If asked about "not yet": open Riley Park from Upcoming visits.
