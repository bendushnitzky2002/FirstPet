## C: Shelter Hand-off

Link: https://dushben.github.io/FirstPet/c/

Backup: on the landing page, press **d** three times (not in a text box). That loads the sample shelter plus Dana Whitfield, who just finished a check for Biscuit, and jumps to the counselor's adopter list. "Reset sample data" on the landing page goes back to the plain sample list.

Live path (about two minutes):

- [ ] Landing: "Two sides of one loop." Click **I'm adopting**.
- [ ] Start screen: "The shelter asks for this, not us. It's step one of four." Point at the step rail. Keep Biscuit, click **Start the check**.
- [ ] Questions: type a name, then Me and a partner, We rent and haven't asked yet, 8 hours, "me mornings, Sam evenings", No plan yet, Tight but doable, The cost. Click **See my result**.
- [ ] Result: "Ready once a few things change," with Biscuit's 6-hour limit and the required cushion. Click **See what the counselor sees**.
- [ ] Readiness summary: "This is what Carla opens at the counter." Point at **Talk about these at the counter**. Click **One-page summary** to show the printable page, then go back.
- [ ] Click **Mark adopted and schedule**, then **Open it as the adopter**: "Two weeks later." Answer Harder than we expected, 11 hours, Under $200, falling on one person, It's crossed my mind, Training. Click **Send to Maple Street**.
- [ ] Click **See what the counselor sees**: the check-in is flagged **Call this week** with the plan versus week two table. Click **All adopters**, then **Week-two check-ins** to show who to call first.

If asked about "not yet": open Riley Park from the adopter list.
