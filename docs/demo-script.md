## B: The Number

Link: https://dushben.github.io/FirstPet/b/

Backup: on the landing page, press **d** three times (not in the ZIP box). That loads a demo check (Durham dog, first reaction "that's a no", answers filled in) and jumps to the number screen. Use it for rehearsal, or if the live flow breaks. **Start over** on the verdict screen clears it.

Live path (about two minutes):

- [ ] Landing: "The only thing we ask first is a ZIP code." Click **27701**, keep **Dog**, click **Show me the number**.
- [ ] The number: "$4,110 for the first year in Durham, and the $2,000 emergency cushion is its own line, marked Required." Point at "Where do these numbers come from?" (sample data, and membership never changes a number). Pick **Honestly, that's a no**: "This is our skeptic."
- [ ] Tune: "Now it becomes their number." Slide weekday hours to 8 and watch the midday walker line appear and the total jump. Pick **1 to 3** nights. Set aside $800, and $300 a month. Then slide hours back to 5 and the walker drops off.
- [ ] Click **See our verdict**: "Ready once a few things change," with one change in dollars: finish the cushion in about four months.
- [ ] "Where are you now?" shows "you said: that's a no." Pick **Ready once these change**. "That move is what we are testing."
- [ ] Click **Save this as a one-page summary**: "Something to bring to the first vet visit, which is Vicky's problem."

If asked about not yet: on the tune screen, set the monthly amount to $100. The verdict says not yet, and still lists what would change it.
