## How this maps to our three tests

- **Owner premium (Prototype A):** Puppr and Vetster Plus show owners do pay subscriptions for help after the pet is home. A tests whether a readiness check earns that same willingness before adoption.
- **Pro membership (Prototype B):** PetDesk is the closest model (businesses pay, owners free). Rover and Good Dog take a cut per transaction, which is the pay-per-use alternative B is testing against.
- **Shelter analytics (Prototype C):** Hushku offers shelters free access. C tests whether shelters would pay for analytics on top of a free check.
- **What nobody in this table does:** give a household a verdict before adoption that can be "not yet."
